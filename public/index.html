A saint for every day

The Saints Calendar

Pick a month, tap a day, and meet a friend of God. Looking for a patron? Try searching “teachers”, “animals”, “students” or “music”.

Colours show the liturgical colour: gold for white feasts, red for martyrs and apostles. Days follow the General Roman Calendar, with a few beloved North American and modern saints on open days (marked “Remembered”). Moveable feasts like Easter, Pentecost and Christ the King change every year, so check your parish calendar too.

Penny Catechism Quest

The classic Penny Catechism (A Catechism of Christian Doctrine), turned into a quest. Answer questions, earn coins, and climb from Pilgrim to Saint. Made for children, for grown-ups going deeper, and for anyone preparing to join the Church through RCIA/OCIA.

  • Bite-size lessons drawn from the catechism’s 370 questions and answers
  • Kid mode and adult mode, with read-aloud
  • Plays in the browser and installs on your phone
